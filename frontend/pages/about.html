Meet Market Atlas

A clearer view of
what fills your basket.

Market Atlas is a learning project with a simple idea. Make everyday grocery prices easier to explore across countries.

01

Explore essentials

Browse groceries by a clearly defined quantity, from a liter of milk to a dozen eggs.

02

Compare places

Look at equivalent items across markets, with transparent units and a shared display currency.

03

Understand your basket

Bring everyday essentials together to see how their combined price varies.

A few things to know

Are these real grocery prices?

No. Every price is sample data written into the HTML to demonstrate the design. No live prices are collected.

Does the site use AI yet?

AI is planned for later. This version contains the HTML pages and CSS styling.

How will the finished site work?

Browser JavaScript will handle interactions. A Node.js backend will validate requests and access PostgreSQL, where products, stores, prices, and user accounts can be stored.

Why can grocery prices differ?

Store location, brands, package sizes, quality, taxes, and promotions can affect the amount paid. Real data will need context and collection dates.
